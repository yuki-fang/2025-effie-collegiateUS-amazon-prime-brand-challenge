# 2025-effie-collegiateUS-amazon-prime-brand-challenge
Selected as one of six semi-finalists in the 2025 Effie Collegiate U.S. Competition out of 50+ teams. Our campaign, Your Prime, Your Way, addressed Gen Z frustrations with algorithm misrepresentation via TikTok, YouTube, Hulu, and Spotify. I led segmentation analysis to identify behavioral patterns and unmet needs, especially among sharers.
